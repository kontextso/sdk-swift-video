# Changelog

## Unreleased

First release of the Kontext Video SDK for iOS, v1 of the *Video Ads API & SDK v2* contract.

* Depends on KontextKit 0.3.0's `KontextKit` product only: the shared device, privacy and StoreKit providers, without the Open Measurement xcframework (the SDK does not measure with OMID yet).
* `KontextVideo.shared.initialize(with:)` (fire-and-forget, `async`, or with a completion), `user` (id, email or its hash, keywords; no demographics, OpenRTB 2.6 deprecated `yob` and `gender`), `regulatory` and `variantId` set later from anywhere; the next request carries them, ads already loaded or fetched stay. `Configuration.coppa`, fixed for the launch.
* `VideoAds(content:playerSize:)` per video, full screen by default; the SDK reads the content playhead through `trackPlayhead(of: AVPlayer)` or `playheadProvider`, when it needs one (the request's `player.playhead`, the `[CONTENTPLAYHEAD]` macro); after the first `show(in:)` the shown view's box is what later requests carry. No player protocol, nothing to start.
* The formats a video will show are declared at `VideoAds(content:formats:)`, each with its options; one `/video/ads` request for all of them is sent at creation and each format's answer waits in its loader. After an overlay or pause ad ends its loader fetches the next one by itself (`Configuration.formatDefaults`, `refetch: .afterEnd`; `.never` for linear), so the next `load()` renders at once. `try await ads.format(.pause)` gives the server's answer for a format as a `VideoAd.Bid` (price, size, duration, ... ; nothing rendered), `ads.format(.linear, options:)` asks the server again, and `bid.load()` renders and returns a `VideoAd` that is ready to present, or throws `VideoAdError` (`noFill`, `formatBusy`, `creativeFailed`, ...). One unshown ad per format at a time; the next one may load while the current one is on screen; `noFill(reason:)` is never retried by the SDK; the app asks again at its next moment. A failed `/config` is retried by every load. `placement.fetch(options)` is the first half of `load`: the server's answer as a `VideoAd.Bid` (`price`, `size`, `duration`, ...) with nothing rendered, for apps that decide before rendering; `bid.load()` is the second half.
* `VideoAd.show(in:)` for overlay and pause ads inside the view the content plays in (an ad with several `sizes` gets the best fit for the view at show: the largest that fits unscaled, else the best scale still ≥ 50 %; `ad.size` is the chosen one), `show()` / `show(from:)` for full-screen linear ads (the presenter found on the key window, or named; `.presenterUnavailable` when none can present), `dismiss()` for the non-linear formats; at most one ad on screen per `VideoAds` (`.anotherAdShowing`); an overlay is removed by the SDK when its `duration` of time on screen elapses (`didEnd(.completed)`; the clock freezes in the background), `OverlayOptions.maxDuration` caps it at request time; `VideoAdDelegate` with exactly one `didEnd(reason:)` per successful show and no "did show" callback (`show` returning is the signal); `didEnd`'s reason is the gesture (the ✕ is `.closed` in every format, a linear ad's Skip `.skipped`, ended by itself `.completed`) and `ad.isCompleted` says whether the creative ran to its end.
* Low Power Mode: the request carries `lowPowerMode` (the server sells the impression as click-to-play); a video ad whose autoplay WebKit refused shows Play and waits for the tap.
* Tracking: every VAST entry fired once by the SDK with the macros substituted, each fire with its own `fid` for server-side dedupe, the click-through carrying the click's `fid` too; `overlayViewDuration` carries the active time on screen (`[VIEWDURATION]`, ms); a page ending itself after `complete` (`ad.close`, reason `complete`) fires no `close`; viewability (`viewable` / `notViewable` / `viewUndetermined`); the Kontext Frame Protocol v2 with the page.
* App Tracking Transparency on request, IDFA only when authorized, consent read from the CMP's IAB keys; `/config` kill switch and retry; `/debug` reporting when the server asks.
* Privacy manifest (`PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy`).
* Tests: 230+ Swift Testing cases, 93 % line coverage, including whole flows through a real `WKWebView`; CI on every push.
